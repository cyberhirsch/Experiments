# Research Briefing: № 07 — Wave Collapse
> Project Status: **Pre-Implementation**
> Domain: **Quantum Mechanics**
> 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> To address various boundary condition requirements, this project is split into multiple versions. Refer to the **v-folders** for specific details on Spectral vs. Grid-based solvers.

## 1. Objective
Visualize the 3D Schrödinger Equation through the lens of quantum tunneling and wave-packet interference.

## 2. Technical Roadmap
- **[v001: SSFM](v001_SSFM/briefing.md):** Split-Step Fourier Method for periodic potentials.
- **[v002: FDTD](v002_FDTD/briefing.md):** Finite-Difference Time-Domain for complex boundaries.
- **Tech:** WebGL2 / WebGPU.

## 3. Design Reference
Following the aesthetic of **№ 02 — Gas Giant (S² FLIP)**:
- **Typography:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic) for headings, *JetBrains Mono* for metrics.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Obsidian (`#070809`) background with high-contrast interference fringes.
- **Layout:** Use the "Scientific Journal" ornamentation (`⟡ ⟡ ⟡`) to separate potential field descriptions from simulation controls.
