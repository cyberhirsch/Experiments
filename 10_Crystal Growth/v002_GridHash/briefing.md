# Research Briefing: № 10 — Crystal Growth [v002: Grid Hash]

Collision detection via a flat spatial hash map. Faster for dense growth where octree traversal overhead is significant.

## 2. Implementation
- **Solvers:** DLA with 3D texture or Buffer-based hash lookup.
- **Visuals:** Dense cluster visualization with high-energy growth facets.
