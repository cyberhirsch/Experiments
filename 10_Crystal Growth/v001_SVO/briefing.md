# Research Briefing: № 10 — Crystal Growth [v001: SVO]
> Path: **Sparse Voxel Octree (SVO)**

## 1. Approach
Uses a hierarchical octree to store the crystalline structure. Highly memory efficient for sparse, branching fractals.

## 2. Implementation
- **Solvers:** DLA with tree-traversal collision detection.
- **Visuals:** Raytraced/Raymarched voxels with faceted normals.

## 3. Design Reference
Consistent with **№ 02 — Gas Giant (S² FLIP)**.
