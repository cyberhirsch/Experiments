# Research Briefing: № 10 — Crystal Growth
> Project Status: **Pre-Implementation**
> Domain: **Fractal Morphogenesis**
> 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> To optimize for different growth densities, this simulation offers two architectural backends. Please refer to the **v-folders** for SVO vs. Spatial Hash specifications.

## 1. Objective
Simulate 3D Diffusion Limited Aggregation (DLA) to create mineral-like fractal architectures that grow procedurally over time.

## 2. Technical Roadmap
- **[v001: SVO](v001_SVO/briefing.md):** Sparse Voxel Octree for memory-efficient branching.
- **[v002: Grid Hash](v002_GridHash/briefing.md):** Uniform Spatial Hash for high-speed dense growth.
- **Tech:** WebGPU Compute / 3D Textures.

## 3. Design Reference
Following the aesthetic of **№ 02 — Gas Giant (S² FLIP)**:
- **Typography:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic) for headings, *JetBrains Mono* for metrics.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Obsidian (`#070809`) background with iridescent or metallic accent colors.
- **Layout:** High-density technical readouts for fractal dimension and growth rate.
