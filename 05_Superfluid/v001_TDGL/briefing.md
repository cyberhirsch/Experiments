# Research Briefing: № 05 — Superfluid [v001: TDGL]
> Path: **Time-Dependent Ginzburg-Landau (Complex PDE)**

## 1. Approach
This version utilizes the TDGL equation to model the order parameter of a superconductor or superfluid. It is a dissipative model that captures the relaxation towards a steady state.

## 2. Implementation
- **Solvers:** Finite Difference on a staggered grid.
- **Visuals:** Phase-to-Color mapping to show vortex orientation and potential gradients.
