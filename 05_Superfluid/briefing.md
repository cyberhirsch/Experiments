# Research Briefing: № 05 — Superfluid Vortices
> Project Status: **Pre-Implementation**
> Domain: **Quantum Fluid Dynamics**
> 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> This experiment explores multiple mathematical paths. Review the **v-folders** within this directory for specific implementation requirements and version-specific roadmaps.

## 1. Objective
Implement a Ginzburg-Landau simulation of quantized vortices in Bose-Einstein condensates. The simulation should capture the spontaneous formation and interaction of vortex filaments in a rotating superfluid.

## 2. Technical Roadmap
- **[v001: TDGL](v001_TDGL/briefing.md):** Complex PDE approach for dissipative relaxation.
- **[v002: Gross-Pitaevskii](v002_GrossPitaevskii/briefing.md):** Spectral method for non-dissipative quantum fluids.
- **Tech:** WebGL2 with 32-bit float textures.

## 3. Design Reference
Following the aesthetic of **№ 02 — Gas Giant (S² FLIP)**:
- **Typography:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic) for headings, *JetBrains Mono* for metrics.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Obsidian (`#070809`) background with Atmospheric Blue (`#7fc7ff`) accents.
- **Layout:** Minimalist stage with technical corner annotations detailing coherence length and winding numbers.
